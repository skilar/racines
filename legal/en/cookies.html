1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences, keep your session active or collect statistical information.

2. Cookies used on this site

https://racinesversailles.com uses the following types of cookies:

  • Technical and functional cookies: necessary for the site to work (session, security, preferences). They do not require consent.

3. Consent and settings

This site only uses technical cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. Under the ePrivacy rules they are exempt from consent, so no cookie banner is required; this policy is published for transparency.

You can also manage cookies from your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
